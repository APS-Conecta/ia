# 2. Editions publish without approval

Accepted, 2026-11-18 (grill for the «IA» app).

An Edition reaches its Readers as soon as its Off hours run finishes, marked as written by AI. Any Editor of its group
may correct or withdraw it afterwards. We chose this for two reasons:

- An approval step would tie the schedule to an Editor being present.
- The facts where a mistake matters most come from the data, not from the Model: the meetings and the documents in
  «Para ti».

## Considered Options

- The Editor approves each Edition first: safer against a small model's mistakes, but Editions would wait on a person.
  Rejected.

## Consequences

- The release quality bar is the safeguard for what the Model writes.
