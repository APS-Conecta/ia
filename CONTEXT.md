# «IA»

These terms keep the meaning gestion-aio's `CONTEXT.md` gives them: «IA local», Model, Team and Cargo.

## Language

**«IA»**:
The suite's own app, next to Assistant, that staff open from the menu to review documents and read Bulletins.
_Avoid_: Asistente, assistant, copiloto, chat

**Assistant** (Asistente):
Nextcloud's own AI app.
_Avoid_: using it for «IA»

### Reviews

**Review** (revisión):
The Model's check of one document and the report it gives: errors, gaps and unclear parts, each with a suggested fix.
_Avoid_: proofreading, corrección, norm check

**Automatic action** (acción automática):
A rule on a folder that has «IA» review the files that arrive or change there.
_Avoid_: workflow, flujo, automation

### Bulletins

**Bulletin** (boletín):
A recurring summary that the Model writes from chosen Sources for a set of Readers.
_Avoid_: informe semanal, digest, newsletter

**Edition** (edición):
One published run of a Bulletin: its shared summary plus each Reader's Personal list.
_Avoid_: issue, número, informe

**Source** (fuente):
A folder, document or calendar that a Bulletin draws on.

**Reading** (lectura):
What the Model takes, once, from one version of a document: a short summary, its expiry date and the Cargos it
concerns.
_Avoid_: ficha, profile, metadata

**Personal list** (lista personal):
The part of an Edition built for one Reader from their own calendar and Cargo, without the Model.

**«Para ti»**:
Everyone's start page: the Personal lists of the Bulletins the person reads, merged.

**Editor group** (grupo editor):
A group whose members jointly own Bulletins and Automatic actions.

**Editor** (editor):
A member of an Editor group.

**Reader** (lector):
A member of a group that a Bulletin is for.

### Operations

**Off hours** (fuera de horario):
The nightly window in which «IA» does its unattended work.
_Avoid_: maintenance window, night batch
